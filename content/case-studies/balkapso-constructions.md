## Overview

BALKAPSO Construction is one of Sikkim's best-known construction and structural engineering firms, based in Gangtok and specialising in seismic retrofitting, structural strengthening and structural assessment. Serving homeowners, builders and institutions across Sikkim's demanding hill terrain, the firm had built its name through referrals. It partnered with Stipill Solutions and Services (Stipill) to build an official website that both search engines and AI assistants could easily understand.

## The Challenge

### No digital footprint in a region where clients search before they call

Despite a strong local reputation, BALKAPSO was difficult to find when prospective clients went looking for help:

- No official website to establish credibility
- Core services invisible to people searching for retrofitting or waterproofing
- Enquiries dependent on referrals and phone calls
- Technical services such as non-destructive testing hard to explain to non-engineers
- Limited visibility on Google Search and Maps, and none in AI-generated answers

Competitors with basic web listings were capturing enquiries that should have gone to the firm.

## The Objective

### A credible, searchable presence that converts local demand into enquiries

The client set out to:

- Establish an official website that reflects its technical authority
- Rank first on Google and appear in AI-generated answers for its brand and core services
- Explain services and project process in plain language
- Generate a consistent flow of qualified leads

## The Solution

### A Next.js website engineered for Google and AI search, structured around how clients look for engineering help

Stipill designed and built the official website, [balkapso.com](https://balkapso.com), on Next.js around the questions clients actually ask. We engineered it for traditional search and AI search from day one.

- **Service pages:** dedicated pages for seismic retrofitting, structural assessment and NDT, and waterproofing and seepage, each targeting its own search intent
- **How We Work:** a clear walkthrough from first conversation to site inspection and delivery, building trust before the first call
- **Service Areas:** location-focused content covering Gangtok and surrounding Sikkim
- **On-page SEO:** keyword-mapped titles and descriptions, canonical URLs, Open Graph tags and a clean URL structure that earned expanded sitelinks in search results
- **Local presence:** consistent name, address and phone details across the website and local listings
- **Performance:** server-rendered Next.js pages with fast load times and a mobile-first layout for users on variable networks
- **Structured data:** JSON-LD schema describing the firm, its services and locations in machine-readable form
- **AI search readiness:** plain-language FAQs and guides on earthquake safety, wall cracks and seepage, written as direct answers that AI assistants can quote and cite

Together, the pages give Google and AI assistants clear, machine-readable signals about what the firm does and where, while giving visitors an easy path to enquire.

## The Impact

### First-page visibility that converts into qualified leads

Within six months of launch, the website had become the firm's most reliable source of new business:

- #1 Google ranking for the brand search "balkapso," with five sitelinks to key service pages
- Cited in AI-generated answers for 6 of 10 tracked local retrofitting and structural assessment queries
- 3,800+ monthly search impressions, up from near zero
- 520+ monthly organic visits
- Top-5 rankings for 9 local service keywords
- 3x increase in monthly enquiries, from 12 to 36
- 41% of enquiries arriving through the website contact form
- 68% of visits from mobile devices

BALKAPSO now shows up first when clients look for it by name, and increasingly when they search for its services, whether on Google or through AI assistants.
